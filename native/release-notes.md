## Added
- Lent and borrowed: open a person (People, in the + menu) for Money between you
- Record You lent or You borrowed, and Settle up with one tap when it is paid back
- The money can be recorded from or into an account, as Lent to someone or Borrowed money
- The People page adds up what you are owed and what you owe, person by person
- Loyalty, membership and gift cards in the Wallet, under your cards
- Scan the card's barcode or pick a screenshot of it: it is read on your phone
- Tap a card at the counter to show its barcode or QR code at full brightness
- A gift card keeps its balance, the date it runs out and its PIN, hidden until you tap Show
- They are kept encrypted on this phone and go with your cards in Export cards and backups
