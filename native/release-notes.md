## Added
- Share a receipt to NormPix, or press and hold its icon: the add form opens over the app you are in
- The Add expense tile and the Add widget's buttons open the same form, and NormPix itself stays closed
## Changed
- Updates now download in a browser tab: tap Download update, open the download and tap Update
- The first time, Android asks you to let your browser install apps; your data stays as it is
- NormPix no longer asks to install apps itself, so Play Protect should stop blocking it
- Updates are no longer downloaded or installed in the background; NormPix tells you when one is out
