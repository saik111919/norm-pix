## Added
- Bill banners on Home and Wallet: due date, how far through the month, and the total due
- Lucide icons: a new choice for every icon in the app (Settings > Appearance > Icons)
- A "Back up your data" reminder on Home when your last backup is old
- Backup reminder setting: every week, 2 weeks or month, or off (Settings > Data & backup)
- Crash log in About: view, share or clear what NormPix kept when it closed unexpectedly
- Home offers the last crash report on the next start
- Add expense, Add income and Transfer from the app icon (press and hold)
- An "Add expense" tile for Quick Settings
- Add and edit screens take soft colors from their category, picture or card (Settings > Colors)
## Changed
- Card bills show what is unpaid of the last statement, not everything owed on the card
- A bill past its due date shows in red as overdue
- Paying a bill opens on the amount that is due
- Needs attention banners use the same two-panel design, each adapted to what it shows
- Overdue reminders show how late they are
- Budget banners show how much is used as a bar, and the amount left or over
- Card expiry banners show the valid-thru date
- Update banners show the size, and the download as a progress bar
- Banner titles can take two lines, and buttons are no longer cut off at large text
- Choosers open as a sheet of rounded option groups; the bank chooser also has search
- Card and account choosers show bank logos and account kinds
- Category picker shows sub-categories as nested rows, with "New category" as a button
- Save in add and edit forms is a floating pill, with Delete and Remind beside it
- Card expiry notices on Wallet use the same banner as Home
- The For field starts on Me, and the People page counts what is not on others as On me
- Date and time fields match the other fields; their sheets show every quick pick and a floating Use button
## Fixed
- Add income shows an arrow again with Lucide icons, instead of a phone
